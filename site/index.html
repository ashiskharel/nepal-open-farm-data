What fits this land

Under a roof

Avocado, fig, and date have import kilograms in the yearbook, not planted hectares, so they are not ranked. Jatamansi, kutki, panchaunle, and yarsagumba are restricted. This desk is not a harvest guide.

Years · वर्ष

2080/81 BS is 2023/24 AD. 2081/82 BS is 2024/25 AD. For these fiscal labels the AD year is the BS year minus 57.

    Pictures from the field

    Save a photo of the crop, a leaf, the path, or the water. The picture stays in this browser. Naming a disease from the photo needs a vision model, and that call costs money, so it is not turned on. Tick what you can see. The note under the photo is a field check, not a lab result.
